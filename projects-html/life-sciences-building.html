Life Sciences Building Rooftop/BIPV Arrays

Completed 2019

UW Solar Team collaborated with Perkins+Will architecture team to develop the schematic plans for a 5 kW building integrated photovoltaic (BIPV) system of vertical shading fins on the southern facade of the Life Sciences Building on the UW Seattle Campus. The 5 kW BIPV system provides enough energy to power the light systems in the main floors. In Autumn 2019, a 100 kW rooftop solar array was completed by Artisan Electric.
