UW Farm resiliency tunnel

Waiting for building permits

The Resiliency Tunnel is a modified hoop house growing structure that will be built adjacent to the Wapato Pond at the UW Farm. The overall structure will take a quarter circle form and provide approximately 3,000 sf to be used as both a growing and learning space. The structure will feature a precast concrete north wall that will act as a thermal mass, regulating temperatures through extreme hot and cold fluctuations. There will be a row of solar panels mounted atop this concrete wall providing the energy needs of the structure, and a water catchment system along the south face to capture water in the rainy season and distribute it to the Wapato Pond during the summer.

The growing space will allow the UW Farm to produce an estimated additional 4,000 pounds of produce per year for the student body and community. The educational space will primarily house educational materials and permit instruction to UW students and the public. Both spaces will support the study of an innovative and sustainable food production system, demonstrating methods for crop protection, thermal regulation in a growing space, and farm waste reduction.
