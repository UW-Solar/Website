Solar canopy over parking lot E-18

Open now

A decade in the making, this 86.4 kW solar canopy is outfitted with 20 Level 2 EV chargers and generates approximately 116,000 kWh per year -- enough to power over 100 UW fleet vehicles. Beyond being responsible for the initial idea, UW Solar managed research, feasibility studies, and grant writing, and was involved throughout the project management process and construction.

The first of its kind, the E18 canopy serves as both an example and the beginning of parking lot-centered solar generation on campus. UW Solar is currently analyzing both the successes and lessons learned from the pilot to examine what expansion would look like across the rest of the E18 lot, across the remainder of the E lots, and ultimately across the entirety of UW's parking lot system. It finished construction in February 2026.
