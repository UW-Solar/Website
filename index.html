About us

UW Solar is a Vertically Integrated Project and student organization that is part of the Urban Infrastructure Lab at the UW.

We participate in the planning, design, and development of solar and related electrification (e.g., electric vehicles, charging, energy storage) projects and information systems on campus, and advise public organizations off-campus (e.g., other colleges, schools, and large public organizations) in this subject matter.

Students from various disciplines and class standings (Freshman to PhD) work together on a diverse set of projects spanning (but not limited to) engineering, architecture, construction management, public policy and administration, business and urban planning.

Campus projects

Solar canopy over parking lot E-18

Open now

A decade in the making, this 86.4 kW solar canopy is outfitted with 20 Level 2 EV chargers and generates approximately 116,000 kWh per year -- enough to power over 100 UW fleet vehicles. Beyond being responsible for the initial idea, UW Solar managed research, feasibility studies, and grant writing, and was involved throughout the project management process and construction.

The first of its kind, the E18 canopy serves as both an example and the beginning of parking lot-centered solar generation on campus. UW Solar is currently analyzing both the successes and lessons learned from the pilot to examine what expansion would look like across the rest of the E18 lot, across the remainder of the E lots, and ultimately across the entirety of UW's parking lot system. It finished construction in February 2026.

Services we provide

  • Site Evaluations and Shading Analysis
  • Photovoltaic (PV) System Preliminary Design
  • Project Development
  • Cost Estimation and Financial Modeling of Solar Arrays
  • Creating and reviewing policy documents with sections for Industry Trends, Federal Regulations, State Law, City Code and UW Policy
  • Procurement of Funding
  • Request for Proposal (RFP) Drafting
  • Contractor Selection
  • Reviewing of Installation and Comissioning
  • Research & Advising
  • Research and Evaluation of Photovoltaic (PV) and Electrical Vehicle (EV) Technologies
  • Compile Databases of Available Technologies
  • Financial Modeling for implementation of PV/EV Technologies
  • Decision Making Models
  • Strategic and Spatial Planning

Meeting details

Meetings are held weekly on Monday and Wednesday, 6-7pm in Gould Hall 012C.
Students of all majors and every class standing are welcome! No experience or application is necessary.

For current UW students

UW Solar is also offered as a 1-2 credit course in the Department of Urban Design and Planning of the College of Built Environments (URBAN 498/598). If you decide to take UW Solar for credit, you must take any of the courses listed for two consecutive quarters. Add codes are available at meetings. UW Solar is an active participant in the UW Sustainability Action Plan, and a proud recipient of the Husky Green Award.
